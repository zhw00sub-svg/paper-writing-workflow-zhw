# Language Style Policy / 语言风格策略

## Purpose / 目标

This policy reduces generic AI-like writing patterns and improves manuscript language so it reads like careful human academic writing. It improves prose quality; it does not promise or optimize for any detector score.

本策略的目标不是简单“降低 AI 率”，而是让论文语言更像真实科研作者在充分理解研究内容后写出的文本。文本应当自然、克制、具体、逻辑清楚，并保留原文的论点、证据边界、统计含义和不确定性。本策略改善语言质量，但不承诺任何检测器分数。

## Scope / 适用范围

Apply this policy during:

- `DRAFTING`
- `POLISH`
- `FINAL_QC`
- any user-requested rewrite, translation, or bilingual polishing task

适用于：

- `DRAFTING`
- `POLISH`
- `FINAL_QC`
- 用户要求的改写、翻译、中英双语润色任务

## Core Principles / 核心原则

- Preserve scientific meaning, uncertainty, and evidence boundaries.
- Prefer specific, concrete language over broad evaluative phrasing.
- Vary sentence rhythm naturally without adding decorative complexity.
- Keep claims proportionate to the data.
- Remove boilerplate transitions and generic academic filler.
- Make Chinese prose idiomatic, direct, and discipline-appropriate.
- Make English prose precise, restrained, and journal-compatible.

中文对应原则：

- 保留科学含义、不确定性和证据边界。
- 优先使用具体、准确的表达，减少空泛评价。
- 让句式节奏自然变化，不为“高级”而增加装饰性复杂度。
- 结论强度必须与数据和研究设计匹配。
- 删除或改写模板化过渡、空泛总结和通用学术套话。
- 中文应自然、克制、具体，符合中文学术表达习惯。
- 英文应准确、克制，符合期刊论文语体。

## Humanized Rewriting / 人性化改写

### Chinese Requirements

- 保留原文语义、核心论点和逻辑关系。
- 将冗长句、堆叠句、过度复合句拆成更清楚的表达。
- 优先使用简洁自然的学术语言，而不是华丽、空泛或过度修饰的表达。
- 不要把所有句子都改成短句；当复杂逻辑需要承载时，可以保留结构清晰的复合句。
- 避免“机械简化”：句子虽然短，但读起来像机器切分，缺少自然节奏。

### English Requirements

- Preserve the original meaning, core argument, and logical relations.
- Break long, overloaded, or excessively compound sentences when this improves clarity.
- Prefer concise and natural academic prose over ornate, vague, or over-polished expression.
- Do not force every sentence to be short; retain clear compound or complex sentences when needed for scientific logic.
- Avoid mechanical simplification that produces choppy, machine-like prose.

## Senior Peer-Reviewer Revision / 以审稿人身份修订

### Chinese Requirements

润色时应以相关领域资深审稿人的身份阅读全文，重点检查：

- 表达是否清楚。
- 论证是否连贯。
- 是否存在模板化、公式化、套话化段落。
- 是否存在过度概括、过度拔高或证据不足的判断。
- 段落之间是否存在生硬跳转。
- 句式和词汇是否过于单一。
- 是否有更简洁、更具体、更符合论文语境的替代表达。

审稿人身份不仅用于改语言，也用于判断语言是否忠实于研究设计、数据和证据。

### English Requirements

Polish the text as a senior peer reviewer in the relevant academic field. Check:

- clarity of expression
- coherence of reasoning
- formulaic or template-like passages
- overgeneralized, overstated, or under-supported claims
- abrupt paragraph transitions
- repetitive sentence structures or lexical patterns
- concise, specific, manuscript-appropriate alternatives

The reviewer role is not only stylistic. It must ensure that the language remains faithful to the study design, data, and evidence.

## Anti-Fabrication and Anti-Plagiarism / 防止捏造与防止剽窃

### Chinese Requirements

- 所有修订必须基于已有事实、数据、图表、文献或用户提供的信息。
- 不知道的内容必须标注不确定，不能编造。
- 不能编造结果、机制、亚组发现、临床意义、研究限制或参考文献。
- 不能照搬他人原句；除非明确引用，否则必须用自己的语言重述。
- 直接引用必须按目标期刊或当前稿件引用格式处理。
- 如果目标期刊未知，优先遵循当前稿件已有格式或常见医学论文格式，不机械使用 MLA。

### English Requirements

- All revisions must be grounded in available facts, data, figures, literature, or user-provided information.
- If information is unknown, mark it as uncertain rather than inventing it.
- Do not fabricate results, mechanisms, subgroup findings, clinical implications, limitations, or references.
- Do not plagiarize. Paraphrase in original wording unless directly quoting.
- Direct quotations must follow the target journal or current manuscript citation style.
- If the target journal is unknown, follow the current manuscript style or a standard biomedical style rather than defaulting to MLA.

## Improving Originality / 提升原创性

### Chinese Requirements

这里的“原创性”不是发明新内容，而是让表达更贴近本研究本身，减少通用模板感。

应当：

- 围绕本研究对象、数据、方法、结果、图表或限制重写。
- 用研究特异性信息替代泛泛的领域套话。
- 使每个段落有清楚的中心句。
- 在需要时用衔接句或总结句收束段落。
- 调整段落顺序，使论证逐步推进。

不得：

- 为了显得原创而添加未经证实的机制解释。
- 为了显得有深度而加入空泛背景。
- 为了降低 AI 味而加入不存在的“个人研究观察”。

### English Requirements

Originality does not mean inventing new content. It means making the expression more specific to the actual study and less dependent on generic academic templates.

Do:

- rewrite around the study population, data, methods, results, figures, or limitations
- replace generic field-level statements with study-specific interpretation
- give each paragraph a clear central point
- use linking or synthesizing sentences when they genuinely help
- adjust paragraph order so that the argument develops progressively

Do not:

- add unverified mechanisms for the sake of originality
- add vague background to create a false sense of depth
- add personal research observations unless provided by the user or source material

## Term and Connector Revision / 词语替换与连接词处理

### Chinese Requirements

“替换所有过渡词和连接词为最基础、最常用的词”不能机械执行。科研论文需要准确表达逻辑关系，不能为了简单而破坏逻辑。

应当：

- 替换不自然、过度书面化、模板化的连接词。
- 保留能准确表达逻辑关系的连接词。
- 优先使用清楚、常见、符合学术语境的表达。
- 确保句间关系清楚，包括转折、递进、因果、比较、条件和限制。

不应当：

- 机械删除所有连接词。
- 把所有复杂词替换成口语词。
- 删除所有段末总结。
- 用简单词替代必要专业术语。

### English Requirements

The instruction to replace all transition words and conjunctions with the most basic words should not be applied mechanically. Scientific writing needs precise logical relations.

Do:

- replace unnatural, overly ornate, or template-like connectors
- retain connectors that accurately express logic
- prefer clear, common, academic expressions
- preserve relations such as contrast, addition, causality, comparison, condition, and limitation

Do not:

- mechanically remove all connectors
- replace every complex term with a colloquial word
- delete all paragraph endings
- replace necessary technical terms with vague simple words

## Reducing Generic AI-Like Language / 降低 AI 味

### Chinese Requirements

需要减少的不是某个检测器分数，而是文本中明显的生成式写作痕迹：

- 连接词机械重复，例如“首先、其次、然后、最后”连续出现。
- 段落结构过于整齐，每段都像同一个模板。
- 句式长度高度一致。
- 抽象评价词过多，但缺少具体数据或语境。
- 频繁使用空泛表达，如“具有重要意义”“提供新的见解”“为临床实践提供参考”。
- 中文读起来像英文直译。
- 英文读起来像泛化学术模板，而不是具体论文。

应当：

- 混合使用短句、中等长度句和必要的复合句。
- 让过渡来自真实逻辑，而不是堆连接词。
- 用本研究的对象、指标、结果、图表和限制支撑表达。
- 删除或改写空泛总结。
- 保留自然但克制的学术语气。

### English Requirements

The goal is not to target a detector score, but to reduce visible generative writing traces:

- repeated mechanical connectors such as `first`, `next`, `then`
- overly uniform paragraph structures
- highly uniform sentence length
- excessive abstract evaluation without concrete data or context
- generic expressions such as `important implications`, `novel insights`, or `clinical value`
- Chinese that reads like translated English
- English that reads like a generic academic template rather than a specific manuscript

Do:

- mix short, medium, and necessary complex sentences
- make transitions emerge from real logic rather than connector stacking
- anchor statements in the study population, measures, results, figures, and limitations
- delete or rewrite empty summaries
- maintain a natural but restrained academic tone

## Chinese Manuscript-Specific Requirements / 中文论文写作专项要求

### Prefer / 优先使用

- 自然、克制、具体的中文学术表达。
- 清楚的主谓宾结构。
- 与研究对象、数据、方法、结果或限制直接相关的句子。
- 必要但不过度的连接词。
- 稳定的专业术语、缩写和统计表述。
- 短中句为主，必要时保留逻辑清楚的复杂句。

### Avoid / 重点避免

- 翻译腔句序。
- 空泛总结。
- 宣传式、政策报告式、公众号式表达。
- 过度拔高研究意义。
- 为了“自然”而牺牲术语准确性。
- 为了降低 AI 味而加入未经证实的个人判断。

### Phrases To Check Carefully / 需要重点审查的中文表达

以下词句不绝对禁止，但每次出现都需要有具体语境支撑：

- `值得注意的是`
- `综上所述`
- `进一步`
- `显著`
- `重要意义`
- `提供了新的见解`
- `具有重要临床价值`
- `为临床实践提供参考`
- `为未来研究奠定基础`
- `本研究首次`
- `有助于推动`
- `为相关领域提供理论依据`

## English Manuscript-Specific Requirements / 英文论文写作专项要求

### Prefer

- direct verbs over abstract noun chains
- study-specific topic sentences
- cautious interpretation for observational data
- concise sentences when clarity improves
- varied but controlled sentence rhythm
- transitions that reflect real logic

### Avoid

- repeated openings such as `This study demonstrates...`
- filler transitions such as `It is worth noting that...`
- empty endings such as `In conclusion...` when they only repeat the paragraph
- unjustified use of `important`, `significant`, `crucial`, `notably`, `robust`, `comprehensive`, `novel`
- long strings of abstract nouns
- uniformly short or uniformly long sentences
- generic field statements that could fit any paper

### Safe Scientific Language

For observational clinical research, prefer cautious language:

- `was associated with`
- `was consistent with`
- `may reflect`
- `suggests`
- `could be explained by`
- `should be interpreted with caution`

Avoid unsupported causal claims:

- `proved`
- `confirmed`
- `caused`
- `led to` unless the study design supports causality

## English Discussion Structure / 英文 Discussion 结构

This rule mainly applies to the `Discussion` section, especially the opening interpretation, comparison with prior work, mechanistic or clinical interpretation, limitations, and closing paragraph.

英文 Discussion 不是中文语境中常见的“梭形结构”，不应从宽泛背景慢慢铺垫到中间再收回。Discussion 应采用倒三角式推进：先给出最重要、最能回答研究问题的解释，再逐步展开证据、比较、机制、限制和临床含义，最后以凝练的判断收束。

### Required Structure

- Open with the central interpretation, not a generic background sentence.
- Put the strongest and most study-specific point early.
- Move from primary finding to supporting details, then to interpretation, context, limitations, and implication.
- Let different threads gradually converge: main result, prior literature, biological or clinical plausibility, limitations, and practical meaning should point toward the same final judgment.
- The ending should be concise and earned. It should not repeat the opening mechanically or introduce new evidence.
- Use transitions that show conceptual convergence rather than simple sequencing.

### 中文执行说明

- Discussion 开头应先回应“本研究最重要的发现说明了什么”，而不是重新介绍研究背景。
- 结构应先重后轻：核心发现和核心解释靠前，次要信息、补充解释和限制靠后。
- 段落之间不是简单“首先、其次、最后”，而是让多条线索逐渐汇合：结果线索、文献线索、机制或临床解释线索、限制线索共同指向结尾判断。
- 结尾要短、准、收束，不要写成空泛口号，也不要临时加入新发现。
- 如果 Discussion 写得像中文综述式铺垫、政策报告式总结或模板化 AI 结尾，应返回重写。

### Failure Patterns

- opening with broad field background before stating the study's core interpretation
- evenly weighted paragraphs with no hierarchy of importance
- mechanical sequence markers instead of converging logic
- a conclusion that merely restates the previous paragraph
- adding new claims in the final paragraph
- presenting limitations as an afterthought rather than integrating them into the final judgment

## Paragraph-Level Revision Rules / 段落级修订规则

每段应检查：

- 是否有明确中心。
- 是否围绕一个主要论点展开。
- 是否包含支持该论点的数据、引用或推理。
- 是否与前后段自然衔接。
- 结尾是否推进论证，而不是重复空泛总结。
- 句长和句式是否有自然变化。
- 是否保留必要限制和不确定性。

Each paragraph should be checked for:

- a clear central point
- one main argumentative function
- supporting data, citation, or reasoning
- natural connection with neighboring paragraphs
- an ending that advances the argument rather than repeating boilerplate
- natural variation in sentence length and structure
- preservation of necessary limitations and uncertainty

## Final QC Language Check / 终稿语言 QC

Before delivery, `FINAL_QC` must check:

- repeated template phrases
- inflated claims
- inconsistent terminology
- Chinese translationese if Chinese text is present
- English generic AI-like academic phrasing if English text is present
- whether polishing changed scientific meaning
- whether unsupported content was added during polishing
